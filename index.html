Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

This app respects your privacy. We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide and improve app functionality.

Information We Collect

Data You Create

The app stores information you enter, such as:

  • Leads and contacts
  • Property interests and details
  • Notes and activity history

This data is stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your private iCloud account.

Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

What we collect:

  • App opens and session duration
  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and crashes
  • Feature usage (which screens you visit, which features you use)
  • General location (country/region level only, not precise location)

What we DO NOT collect:

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • The actual content of your leads, properties, or notes
  • Precise location data
  • Any personally identifiable information

Purpose:

  • Understand which features are most used
  • Identify and fix crashes
  • Improve app performance and user experience

All analytics data is:

  • Anonymous and aggregated
  • Cannot be tied back to you personally
  • Processed by Google Firebase (see Third-Party Services below)

iCloud Sync (Optional)

  • Your app data is synced across your devices using your private iCloud account
  • Data is stored in Apple's CloudKit infrastructure
  • All synced data is encrypted by Apple
  • Only you can access your synced data through devices signed into your iCloud account
  • We do not have access to your iCloud data

You control iCloud sync:

  • iCloud sync only works if you're signed into iCloud on your device
  • You can disable iCloud sync by signing out of iCloud in Settings
  • Disabling iCloud keeps data local to your device only

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Collect your personal information (name, email, phone, address)
  • Access the content of your leads, properties, or notes
  • Track your precise location
  • Sell your data
  • Share your data with third parties for their own purposes
  • Show personalized advertisements
  • Link analytics data to your identity

How We Use Information

Your Content Data

  • Stored locally on your device
  • Synced to your iCloud (if you enable sync)
  • Never accessed, viewed, or shared by us

Analytics Data

  • To understand app usage patterns
  • To identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • To improve features and performance
  • To make decisions about future development

Analytics data is anonymous and cannot be connected to you or your content.

Data Sharing

Your Content Data

Your leads, properties, and notes are never shared with anyone.

Analytics Data

Anonymous usage analytics are processed by Google Firebase Analytics.

  • Purpose: Sync your data across your devices
  • What's shared: Your app data
  • Privacy: Encrypted, private to your account

  • Purpose: Improve the app

Data Storage and Security

  • App sandboxing
  • iOS encryption

iCloud Storage

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Private database
  • Protected by Apple ID

Analytics Storage

  • Stored on Firebase servers
  • Anonymous and aggregated

Data Retention

Your Content

  • Remains until deleted by you
  • Removed when app is deleted

Analytics

  • Retained up to 14 months

Your Rights and Control

  • Delete your data anytime
  • Disable iCloud sync
  • Control your iCloud data

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

International Data Transfers

Data may be processed internationally via Apple and Google infrastructure.
